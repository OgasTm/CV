# Public datasets, benchmarks, corpora and open tooling for the IRSA LLM agent / conversation layer (verified 2026-11-20)

Method note: licences were checked against the actual LICENSE file in each GitHub repo (fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com) or the Hugging Face (HF) API `cardData.license` field on 2026-11-20. Where neither existed, the item is marked "no licence file". The GitHub REST API and some sites (nrc.gov, iaea.org/about/terms-of-use) refused automated requests, so some items are confirmed through search results or mirrors only. These are flagged.

Legend for "IRSA module": KB = RAG knowledge base; RULES = versioned rule engine authoring; BENCH = IRSA-Bench seed or template; SIM = patient simulator; JUDGE = LLM-as-judge or eval harness; GUARD = guardrails or output verifier; TOOLS = dose-engine tool exposure; LLM = base model.

## 1. Openly accessible radiation-protection / nuclear-medicine text corpora, and existing NM or radiation-safety QA datasets and benchmarks

### Takeaway
There is no public, licensed radiation-safety patient-dialogue dataset or benchmark. IRSA-Bench must be built. The best seed material is public-domain US NRC text, CC BY 4.0 EANM/SNMMI guidelines, free-to-read (but copyright-retained) IAEA and ICRP documents, and the HPS "Ask the Experts" archive (more than 15,000 answered questions, all rights reserved, permission needed). Only NRC text, eCFR text and CC BY guideline articles can go into a redistributable KB without asking. IAEA, ICRP, HPS, SNMMI and NHS-trust text needs permission for anything beyond internal reading or paraphrase.

### Inferences
- The only sources IRSA can ingest, change and redistribute without permission are NRC/eCFR (public domain), the CC BY 4.0 EANM/SNMMI 177Lu-PSMA guideline, and CC BY research papers. IAEA SRS-63/SSG-46 and ICRP 94/103/128/140 are free to read but need permission to reuse. Because IAEA TC is the sponsor, a formal IAEA permission (including translations) is probably easy to get and should be requested early. ICRP permission goes through annals@icrp.org.
- Regulatory thresholds (5 mSv/1 mSv in §35.75, the German 3.5 µSv/h at 2 m, the Japanese release conditions, the Swiss <10 µSv/h at 1 m) differ by jurisdiction. This supports the earlier recommendation to encode them in a versioned rule engine, not to retrieve them via RAG. The DG-8061 draft versus Rev. 1 pair is a ready-made "rule-version drift" test case.
- The HPS ATE archive is the best real-world source of lay phrasing (caregivers, pregnancy, pets, travel, work). The safe route is to ask HPS for permission to use paraphrased question stems as IRSA-Bench prompts, without reproducing answers. If permission is refused, generate paraphrased question templates by hand from the category topics.
- Koller et al. MedChat comes from the same group as the IRSA concept. Its EN/DE question set and RAG pipeline could be relicensed internally with little effort and extended to patient-facing questions.

### Gaps
- No HPS terms on automated access or text-and-data mining were found. Licensing has to be cleared by email.
- IAEA terms-of-use page could not be fetched directly (HTTP 402/403); the wording comes from a mirror and catalogue notices.
- Per-guideline licences for EANM I-131 and 177Lu-DOTATATE guidelines and the SNMMI procedure standards were not checked.
- Copyright terms for each NHS trust leaflet were not checked.
- "DG-8070" could not be found. RG 8.39 Rev. 2 final status is unconfirmed after the April 2023 draft.
- The current consolidated text of the German Richtlinie Strahlenschutz in der Medizin and the current Japanese MHLW annex thresholds were not retrieved.
- No public radiation-safety patient-dialogue benchmark was found on HF or GitHub (searches on HF for radioiodine, theranostic, radiopharmaceutical, dosimetry, health-physics and lutetium returned nothing relevant).

## 2. Patient-dialogue and interactive medical benchmarks (incl. clarification, sycophancy, prompt injection, medical safety red-teaming) usable as IRSA-Bench templates

### Takeaway
Permissive (MIT / Apache / CC BY / CC BY-SA) templates exist for each IRSA-Bench component. Use HealthBench (rubrics), MedDialogRubrics (must-ask rubrics, licence unclear), MediQ and AgentClinic (interactive asking), τ²-bench (Pass^k, user simulator, tool and policy grounding), MedCalc-Bench and MedMCP-Calc (calculator and tool correctness), MedPRESS (multi-turn pressure; gated, research-only), AgentDojo and InjecAgent (indirect prompt injection), and MedSafetyBench (harmful requests). PatientAgentBench is the closest architectural match but is CC BY-NC, so it cannot be used commercially.

### Inferences
- For a commercially usable IRSA-Bench, the cleanest base is τ²-bench (MIT) plus HealthBench rubric format (MIT) plus AgentDojo injection suites (MIT), all runnable in Inspect (MIT). PatientAgentBench (CC BY-NC), MedPRESS (gated, research-only), Q4Dx (CC BY-NC-ND article, no repo licence) and MedDialogRubrics (no licence) should inspire the design, but their data should not be shipped.
- Copy the MedDialogRubrics "atomic fact" patient grounding and the MediQ hidden-information pattern in the IRSA simulator. Each simulated household gets a fact sheet (ages, pregnancy, bed sharing, commute length, pets, work). Must-ask rubrics then follow from which facts the rule engine needs, which matches the "engine-driven clarification" recommendation.
- MedCalc-Bench's tolerance-band scoring and MedMCP-Calc's tool-selection grading carry over directly to "numbers only from engine output". Score (a) whether the right engine tool was called with the right slots, and (b) whether every number in the reply matches engine output exactly, with no tolerance for invented numbers.
- No benchmark targets jurisdiction-switching or rule-version drift. That is new IRSA-Bench content, and the regulatory corpora in Section 1 can seed it (NRC Rev. 1 vs DG-8061; DE vs CH vs JP release criteria).

- MediQ README and exact dataset sizes were not readable this session.

- No licence was found for MedDialogRubrics, InjecAgent, ClarQ, ClariQ, EHR-ChatQA, MedQDx, LLMPatientSafety, MedCalc-Pro or CV-MedBench. Ask the authors.
- No SycEval code or data repo was found.
- No medical-domain prompt-injection benchmark with released data was found (the npj Digital Surgery 2026 VLM study was seen in the earlier session; its data availability was not checked).

### Inferences
- For an IAEA TC prototype that may be deployed in many member states, Gemma 4 E4B/12B or Qwen3.5 4B-9B (both Apache-2.0, 140+ and 201 languages) are the lowest-risk defaults for on-device or self-hosted use. MedGemma 1.5 4B is worth benchmarking for medical tone, but its HAI-DEF terms add regulatory and liability clauses. The IRSA design, where numbers and decisions come only from the deterministic engine and rules, makes medical fine-tuning less important than instruction-following, structured output and multilingual quality.
- Benchmark all candidate models on IRSA-Bench with constrained decoding enabled (XGrammar or Outlines). Small models often fail free-form JSON but pass under grammar constraints.

### Gaps
- No public benchmark of these models on radiation-safety patient communication exists.
- The Apertus language count and the Phi-4 multilingual quality were not checked.
- The current HAI-DEF terms were read only through search snippets (dated Nov 2024 copies). Re-read the live terms before deciding.
- The Llama 4 licence text was not read this session.
